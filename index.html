Questions

Before you come in.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

How do I know if my phone is worth repairing?

Bring it in. We check the fault and tell you the repair price and time before we start, so you can decide.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the fault and the part needed. We give you a time before we begin. Call us with your phone model and the problem and we will tell you what to expect.

Will I lose my data?

Many repairs do not touch your data, but a backup is always safer. Back up before you come, or ask us to help you back up and transfer your files.

My phone got wet. What should I do?

Switch it off straight away and do not charge it. Remove the case and SIM card, wipe the outside and bring it in as soon as you can. The sooner it is checked, the better the chance of saving it.

Can you help me choose a new phone?

Yes. Tell us your budget and what you use your phone for, such as photos, games, work or just calls and messages, and we will show you suitable options in the shop.

Do you sell accessories?

Yes. We stock cases, screen protectors, chargers, cables, earphones and power banks. Call ahead if you need something for a particular model.

Where is the shop and when is it open?

We are at Lulu Ruwi Souq, Muscat. Opening hours are not listed on this page, so please call or send a WhatsApp message before you travel.
